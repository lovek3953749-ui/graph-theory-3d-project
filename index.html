SPARSE NETWORK DEFAULT

Adjacency list

SPACE O(V + E)

Each vertex stores its neighboring vertices and edge weights. Neighbor iteration is proportional to the degree of the vertex.

BEST USE Sparse networks, BFS/DFS, and most practical network problems.

EVALUATION PLAN

Five validation dimensions

01CorrectnessCompare algorithm outputs with hand-worked examples and expected answers.
02CoverageTest connected/disconnected graphs, isolated vertices, cycles, and multiple routes.
03PerformanceRecord runtime for increasing V and E; do not claim unmeasured benchmarks.
04UsabilityKeep the graph readable, label units, and explain what each weight means.
05LimitationsState whether data is simulated, estimated, incomplete, or stale.
